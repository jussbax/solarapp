## What the copywriter wanted to write and could not (ask the owner)

1. "The fan does not even stop" on Brownouts: needs the hybrid inverter's switch-over time from the maker's datasheet.
2. "…and answers the phone afterwards" on About and in the FAQ: needs who answers after switch-on, on which number, and how fast.
3. One typhoon line in the FAQ: needs the mounting system's rated wind speed and whether the PEE designs to the NSCP wind zone.
4. "Your power is off for about an hour on the second morning" in step 5: needs the installation-day outage length (the proposal's installation_outage_hours setting).
5. "Then keeps producing for N more years" and the hidden fifth tile: needs the panel performance warranty years entered in the profile.
6. A first real voice ("the three-roof family's brownouts"): needs each customer's yes to one sentence.
7. Towns on the three cards: needs each customer's agreement.
8. "The bill went from about ₱X to about ₱Y" on the rib-roof and hip-roof captions: nobody has said what either bill became.
9. "Most evenings there is no brownout at all": there is no brownout-frequency fact for Laguna and Batangas.
