About us

A solar engineering company that measures before it designs.

PL Development Inc. designs and installs rooftop solar for homes from Pila, Laguna, across Laguna and Batangas.

We started with a simple complaint: solar quotes that came from a satellite photo and a price list, and systems that never made what the brochure promised. So we do it the slow way. We go to the house, put a test panel and meters on the roof, read the sun and the shade, and size the system to what that roof really makes and what that house really uses. The proposal that follows is exact. It can come out lower or higher than the first estimate, and it shows you why.

Who you will meet

runs the company and is on every roof visit and every proposal.

Electrical plans are signed and sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer, (PRC No. ). Our crew of a team lead, a skilled technician and helpers installs in one or two days and leaves the roof the way it found it, with sealed fasteners and no open holes.

How we work

  • One engine from estimate to proposal. The free estimate on this website, the roof check after the visit and the proposal all come from the same calculation, so the numbers agree with each other.
  • Honest about the battery. A battery is for brownouts and comfort; it adds little to the savings. We show both prices and let you choose.
  • Everything in the price. Delivery, installation, testing, the sealed plans, the permit and the final inspection, the ERC certificate of compliance and the net metering application with your electric company.
  • Warranties in writing, printed on every proposal, from the maker on the equipment and from us on the installation.
