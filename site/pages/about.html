Why we measure

We started with a simple complaint.

Solar quotes came from a satellite photo and a price list, and the systems never made what the brochure promised. So we measure first. We go to the house, put a test panel and meters on the roof, read the sun and the shade, and size the panels and the battery to what that roof really makes and what that house really uses. The proposal that follows is exact to your roof; where it differs from the first estimate, it shows you why.

How we work

Installers sell you panels. We hand you the engineering.

  • One calculation from estimate to proposal. The free estimate on this website and the proposal after the visit come from the same calculation, so the numbers agree with each other.
  • Straight about the battery. The battery buys comfort and backup through brownouts; the panels bring the bill down. We show both prices and you choose.
  • Everything in the price, installed by our own crew. Delivery, installation, testing, the sealed plans, the permit and the final inspection, the ERC certificate of compliance (the net metering certificate) and the net metering application with your electric company.
  • Warranties in writing, printed on every proposal, from the maker on the equipment and from us on the installation.
