Why we measure

We started with a simple complaint.

Solar quotes that came from a satellite photo and a price list, and systems that never made what the brochure promised. So we do it the slow way. We go to the house, put a test panel and meters on the roof, read the sun and the shade, and size the panels and the battery to what that roof really makes and what that house really uses. The proposal that follows is exact. It can come out lower or higher than the first estimate, and it shows you why.

How we work

One engine, everything in the price.

  • One engine from estimate to proposal. The free estimate on this website, the roof check after the visit and the proposal all come from the same calculation, so the numbers agree with each other.
  • Honest about the battery. A battery is for brownouts and comfort; it adds little to the savings. We show both prices and let you choose.
  • Everything in the price. Delivery, installation, testing, the sealed plans, the permit and the final inspection, the ERC certificate of compliance (the net metering certificate) and the net metering application with your electric company.
  • Warranties in writing, printed on every proposal, from the maker on the equipment and from us on the installation.
