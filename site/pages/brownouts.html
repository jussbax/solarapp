What a battery does, plainly.

In a brownout

The switch-over is automatic. A battery sized to your evening carries lights, fans, the refrigerator, TV and wifi through a typical night; running aircon shortens that. The estimate tells you whether yours holds a typical night or how many hours of your evening it carries, and we size the battery to what you want to keep on.

Every other day

The battery stores the afternoon's extra solar and spends it after sunset, so less of your evening comes from the grid. The rest of the surplus is still credited on your bill under net metering.

What it costs

The battery is roughly a third to nearly half of a hybrid system's price. It buys comfort and backup more than savings, and the estimate shows both prices, with and without the battery, so you can decide.
