    Your questions

    Answered before you ask.

    What happens in a brownout?

    With a battery, it takes over by itself: lights, fans, the fridge, TV and Wi-Fi run through a typical evening while the rest of the street waits for the grid; running aircon shortens that. Without a battery, a net-metered system switches off during a brownout, as the safety rules require, and restarts on its own when the grid returns.

    How does net metering work?

    Power you do not use by day goes to the grid, and your electric company credits it on your bill at its generation rate (the rate it pays for power, lower than the rate you pay per kWh). That is why a bill does not reach zero, and why we size the panels to what you use rather than to the roof.

    Who handles the permits and the papers?

    We do. The electrical plans, the permit and the final inspection, the ERC certificate of compliance and the net metering application with your electric company are in the price. You sign two forms.

    What if we move house?

    The system stays with the house. Net metering is tied to the service connection, so the new owner continues it with the electric company; we help with the paperwork.

    What does the installation do to the roof?

    The rails clamp to the roof framing through the sheet with sealed fasteners. Installation takes one or two days and leaves no open holes. Workmanship is covered by our own warranty.

    What about typhoons?

    The rails are fixed through the sheet to the roof framing with sealed fasteners, on plans a Professional Electrical Engineer signs. Our oldest installation, from 2023, has stood through every typhoon season since.
