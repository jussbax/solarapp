How it works

Your meter runs both ways.

  1. By day The panels power the house first. Whatever is left goes to the grid through a two-way meter.
  2. At night The house draws from the grid as before.
  3. On the bill Your electric company credits every kWh you sent at its generation rate, the part of your tariff it pays for power. The fixed charges and the evening hours stay, so a small bill still comes, and the estimate shows about what yours would be.
  4. The papers We file the net metering application, the ERC certificate of compliance (the net metering certificate) and the meter request. You sign two forms. Between switch-on and the two-way meter the panels already cut your daytime bill.

Brownouts

Want lights in a brownout too?

Add a battery and the house runs through brownouts. Without one, the panels pause while the grid is down, as the safety rules require, and restart on their own when it returns. The estimate shows both prices, and a battery can be added later.
