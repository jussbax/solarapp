Privacy notice

What we collect, and why.

This notice covers the free estimate on this website and the roof visit you can book from it. It follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

The estimate

To work out an estimate we use the answers you give: what you want from solar, your town or the location your phone shares, your monthly consumption or bill amount, and when you use power. The estimate is calculated on our server and is not tied to your name. We keep a count of estimates (town, size, price, the link you came from) to see how the page is used. A random identifier is stored in your browser only to limit how many estimates one browser can run in an hour; it is not a tracking cookie and we do not use advertising trackers on this website.

Booking a roof visit

If you book a visit we keep your name, your mobile number or Messenger name, the address or landmark you give, the location, the estimate you saw and the time you prefer to be reached. We use them only to arrange and carry out the visit, to prepare your roof check and proposal, and to answer your questions. We never sell them. The only others who handle them are the services that process them for us: the e-mail service that tells us a booking arrived, and the encrypted off-site backup. If you tell us the link you came from (for example a Facebook ad), we keep that too, to know which of our posts people find useful.

How long we keep it

  • A booking that does not lead to a visit: your name, contact and address are erased after twelve months from our last contact. Backups age out within a month after that.
  • A customer's assessment, proposal and installation records: kept for as long as the system is under our warranty, and as the tax rules require for the sale.
  • Estimate counts: kept without names, and without a precise location, for ninety days.

Where it lives

Your details are stored on our own server in the Philippines, behind a login that only our staff hold, and reached only over encrypted connections. Backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Every login and every deletion is recorded.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on Messenger or call the number in the footer and we will do it within a few working days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission.
